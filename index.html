What we do

Services

Five things, done properly. Work with us from Vancouver, Toronto or anywhere with Wi-Fi.

  1. 01

    Brand identity & logos

    Marks people recognise at a glance, plus the colours, type and rules that keep them consistent.

  2. 02

    Websites & web apps

    Fast, mobile-first sites that turn visits into bookings, up to member portals with logins and dashboards.

  3. 03

    3D, motion & VFX

    Interactive 3D, animated heroes and film-grade compositing, like the dive at the top of this page.

  4. 04

    Games & interactive stories

    Browser and mobile games made in-house, from the pixel art to the code. Our first, Khizesh, is playable now.

  5. 05

    Illustration & live art

    Hand-drawn illustration, collage and live caricature that gives people something to take home.
